Privacy Policy for DetoxPet

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Developer: RLO Studio

RLO Studio built the DetoxPet app as a freemium digital wellbeing application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use DetoxPet.

1. App Usage & Screen Time Data (Local Processing Only)

DetoxPet requests Android Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) and runs a local foreground service to monitor screen time and detect when you open self-selected blacklisted apps during active focus sessions.

  • All screen time statistics and foreground app checks are processed 100% locally on your device.
  • RLO Studio does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or transmit your app usage history or installed app list off your device.

2. Advertising & Consent Management (Google AdMob)

DetoxPet uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its certified ad technology partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, and interaction data to serve and measure ads.

  • Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are presented with a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (UMP) dialog upon first launch to manage their ad personalization preferences.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

DetoxPet offers optional digital items and upgrades (such as the Supporter Pack, Emergency Kit, Treat Bag, Mystery Pet, and Mystery Color) processed securely through Google Play Billing. RLO Studio does not receive or store your credit card or payment details; Google provides only purchase confirmation tokens so the app can unlock your items.

4. Local Storage

Your pet's health, coins, treats, unlocked pets, color themes, streaks, and sleep schedule preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's internal storage. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

5. Children's Privacy

DetoxPet is not directed at children under the age of 13, and RLO Studio does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or DetoxPet, please contact RLO Studio at: rlo.studio.dev@gmail.com
